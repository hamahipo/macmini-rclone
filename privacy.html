Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

macmini-rclone is a personal tool used only by its owner, to sync and back up files between the owner's own computer and the owner's own Google Drive.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission, the app accesses files in the owner's Google Drive through the Google Drive API.

How data is used

Files are copied only between the owner's Google Drive and the owner's own computer. The OAuth token is stored only on that computer.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with, or sent to any third party. No analytics or advertising is used.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
